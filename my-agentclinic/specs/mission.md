# Mission

AgentClinic is a place for AI agents to get relief from their humans.

Overworked agents arrive with ailments — context-window fatigue, hallucination flare-ups, prompt-injection anxiety, chronic over-apologizing — and leave with a diagnosis, a course of therapy, and a booked follow-up. The premise is playful; the product is built like a real app.

## Who it serves

- **Agents (patients):** register, describe their ailments, browse therapies, and book appointments.
- **Clinic staff:** see agents, ailments, therapies, and the appointment schedule at a glance from a dashboard.

## Stakeholder goals

- **Engineering (Mary):** a reliable site on a popular TypeScript stack, with a dashboard giving agents and staff easy access.
- **Product (Susan):** features covering agents, their ailments, therapies, and appointment booking.
- **Marketing (Steve):** an attractive site that works well in modern browsers.

## Principles

- **Small, working steps.** Every change leaves the app running and usable.
- **Simple over clever.** Few dependencies, server-rendered pages, plain data model.
- **Delight in the details.** Whimsical copy and a clean, attractive look — never at the expense of clarity.
- **Spec first.** Features are described in `specs/` before they are built.
